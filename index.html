Webhook Delivery Platform

Node.jsFastifyRedisBullMQPostgreSQL

Event-driven webhook delivery engine using Fastify, PostgreSQL/Drizzle, and Redis/BullMQ. Persists events and per-endpoint deliveries transactionally, enforces distributed per-endpoint rate limits via Bottleneck without burning retries, signs payloads with HMAC-SHA256 using AES-256-GCM encrypted secrets, and auto-disables failing endpoints after jittered exponential backoff.
